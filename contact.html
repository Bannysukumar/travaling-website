Frequently Asked Questions

How can I book a flight?

You can easily book a flight through our website by visiting the Flights page, entering your travel details, and following the booking process.

What is your cancellation policy?

Our cancellation policy varies depending on the type of booking. Generally, cancellations made 24 hours before the travel date are eligible for a full refund.
